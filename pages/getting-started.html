Getting Started

Here are the steps to get started with UMADL:

  • Step 1: Download UMADL. There are 3 finished builds available (OSX, Linux, and Windows), or you can download the repo and compile it yourself.
  • Step 2: Please check the license, as UMADL is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0.
  • Step 3: Read this documentation.
