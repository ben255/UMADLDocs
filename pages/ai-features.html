AI Features

UMADL is planned to feature deep AI integration, starting with Clawbot. Our goal is to streamline your workflow by transforming your inputs into completed tasks automatically.

Security & Sandboxing

For security purposes, the AI operates within an isolated sandbox environment. This ensures that the AI can only access and edit authorized files, keeping the rest of your project and system secure.

Model Integration

While Clawbot is the initial focus, we are actively exploring additional integrations, including the PewDiePie AI model. Furthermore, UMADL will offer full flexibility by allowing you to seamlessly add and configure your own custom AI models.


Note: These features are currently planned and subject to change as development continues.
