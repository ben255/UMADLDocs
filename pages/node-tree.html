Node Tree Structure

In UMADL, the internal file and folder structure is visually and functionally represented as a node tree. This makes mapping connections and relationships highly efficient.

Node Types

The hierarchy is broken down into three specific types of nodes:

  • Master Nodes: These act as folders. They group related assets together and form the top-level structure of your hierarchy.
  • Root Nodes: These represent individual files. They exist within Master Nodes and act as the primary container for your data.
  • Child Nodes: These represent the internal file data. They belong to Root Nodes and map out the granular details, properties, or variables inside a specific file.

Note: By mapping these nodes together, the AI can understand exactly how different pieces of your project interact with each other.

We dont write code anymore. We control the flow of data, manage it and tell the individual child nodes what data we expect back and where to send it. How is not our concern other than the specific backends, middleware and imports or modules we choose to use.
