Writing code is a lot like writing a book, we all know what story we want to write. What words to use and how we want it to end. But the middle part of the book usually is what takes time. Getting from the beginning to the start. Now we could tell AI to write this book with the beginning in mind and the ending in mind but the story wont be ours.

This is where UMADL will come into effect, It will be a way to build everything without having to write. You can map the connections in the story. The relationships of characters, even the personality of all the characters. But the rest AI can do for us. All the words it takes to write that you could do and you know what you want to write but its just more efficient to let AI do it.

This tool is not with learning in mind but in fast execution and development, everyone needs to learn the alphabet before you even can begin, then the basics of writing stories. What, When, Who, Where and Why?
